Why road safety matters

Source: Ministry of Road Transport & Highways (MoRTH), Road Accidents in India 2023. Two-wheeler riders were about 44% of deaths and pedestrians over 20%.

What kills on Indian roads

    Share of 2023 road deaths linked to each factor. Factors overlap, so the bars do not add up to 100%. Helmet non-use killed 54,568 riders and seat-belt non-use killed 16,025 people.

    The golden hour

    The first hour after a crash decides who survives. Call 108 or 112 right away.

    Under the Good Samaritan rules (Section 134A, Motor Vehicles Act), you can help an injured person without being forced to pay, stay or become a witness.

    Rules and penalties

    Maximum penalties under the Motor Vehicles (Amendment) Act, 2019. Tamil Nadu has kept some fines lower than the central rates, so the challan you receive can differ. Check the Tamil Nadu Police or Parivahan e-Challan portal.
